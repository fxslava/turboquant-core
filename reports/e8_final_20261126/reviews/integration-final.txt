Metadata and forward

  its base builder provides CPU query lengths and sequence lengths. Async

  consumes the already staged scheduler accepted-count copy. The E8 builder
  refuses paths that would select device sequence lengths, including parallel
  drafting. Plans stop at num_actual_tokens before SP dummy rows can reach
  the table, and dummy runs use empty plans. Metadata and its H2D offset upload
  are shared by the layers in an attention group without a new D2H transfer.

  and aligns each fused offset table to one 32-byte burst. The reader supports

  inverse rotation applied. Other requests use dense attention after gathering
  and dequantizing cache-basis packets. Device fallback correctness remains a
  physical-server verification item.
